ABA KHQR “Scan to pay” modal

The real PayWay sandbox template6_color image is used in both captures. The modal is a single 520 px column: header/status pill, the PayWay image at its natural 443 × 301 size on a white panel, amount, reference, expiry, then the actions. The image is not cropped or stretched; its rendered aspect ratio is exactly its natural 1.47 aspect ratio. The QR itself is 144 × 144 in the delivered image.

What changed

  • generate-qr now sends qr_image_template: "template6_color" as the last signed QR field, after lifetime.
  • AbaConfig::QR_IMAGE_TEMPLATE is the only production template literal; the env/config template override was removed.
  • lifetime is enforced to at least 3 minutes in the config, AbaConfig, and AbaQrService.
  • Fresh sandbox smoke test after the change (separate from the captures above): POST /generate-qr returned HTTP 200 with PayWay status.code = "0" (Success.), tran_id = K261106022521501765; the returned 443 x 301 image decodes to a 144 x 144 QR ending 630418C5.
  • The presentation component is the scoped frontend/src/components/ABA/KhqrPaymentModal.jsx + .css; KhqrModal still owns polling, callbacks, success delay, and order completion.
  • No polling, callback, or order logic changed. Props passed from POS.jsx are unchanged.

End states (1366 × 768 preview)

Scan verification note: a physical phone scan is not available in this environment, so the full screenshots were decoded with pyzbar as the scan proxy. Both decoded payloads exactly match the sandbox qr_string (ending 63046BD6). ZXing does not detect the dense, 144 px template6_color QR (even from a 4× crop); pyzbar is the evidence used here.
